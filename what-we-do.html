Core Work

QNONCE gives every product a verifiable digital identity.

The platform is built around one clear goal: make genuine products easier to prove and suspicious product activity easier to identify.

Product Authentication

Every product can receive a unique QR-based identity connected to its product information, batch details, brand record, and verification status.

Customer Verification

Customers can scan a QNONCE-powered QR code and instantly understand whether the product appears authentic, suspicious, blocked, or missing from the system.

Counterfeit Risk Monitoring

Brands can track suspicious activity signals such as repeated scans, invalid QR checks, customer reports, blocked product status, and unusual verification behavior.

Product Traceability

Product details, batch records, QR identity, verification history, and issue reports stay connected to support better visibility across product activity.

Brand Value

Built to protect trust at product level.

Counterfeit products can damage customer confidence and brand reputation. QNONCE helps companies create a stronger verification experience so customers can check authenticity and brands can review risk signals.
