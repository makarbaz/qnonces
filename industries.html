Why sectors matter

Different industries face different counterfeit risks.

A fake medicine can create safety concerns. A fake cosmetic can damage customer confidence. A fake spare part can affect performance. QNONCE provides a flexible verification layer that can support different product categories without making the customer experience complicated.
