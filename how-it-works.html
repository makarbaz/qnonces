Process

From product identity to customer verification.

QNONCE keeps the authentication journey simple for brands and customers while still supporting product-level visibility and counterfeit risk monitoring.

01
Brand Side

Register the Product

The brand adds product information such as product name, category, batch details, manufacturing information, and product status. This becomes the product’s digital verification record.

  • Product details
  • Batch information
  • Brand and category data
02
QR Identity

Generate Secure QR Verification

QNONCE creates a unique QR-based identity connected to the product record. This QR identity becomes the customer-facing verification gateway for that product.

  • Unique QR identity
  • Product record connection
  • Verification-ready experience
03
Customer Side

Customer Scans and Verifies

The customer scans the QR code and receives a clear verification result. The experience can show whether the product is authentic, suspicious, blocked, or missing from the system.

  • Instant verification result
  • Mobile-friendly scan experience
  • Product issue reporting option
04
Monitoring

Brand Reviews Reports and Risk Signals

Brands can monitor product verification activity, review customer reports, track suspicious signals, and maintain a clearer view of product trust across different markets.

  • Customer-reported issues
  • Suspicious scan signals
  • Product-level verification history
